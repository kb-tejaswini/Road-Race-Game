HOW TO PLAY

Race a field of 15 riders on real roads around the world, from Bali to the Arctic. Every road is open from the start. Your position, the gap to the riders ahead and behind, and the live standings show while you race.

Two players. Pick 2 PLAYERS on the road-select screen for split-screen on one keyboard. Player 1 (left screen): arrow keys, punch with . or Right Shift, kick with / or Enter. Player 2 (right screen): W A S D, punch with Z or Space, kick with X. You can fight each other too.

Fighting. Pull up beside a rider and punch or kick. Every hit you land knocks them off their bike. Rivals also fight each other. If you hold your speed, their hits only make you wobble. If you slow down, a hit can knock you off. Some riders carry a club: knock them off and it's yours.

The cop chases whoever is nearest. If he reaches any rider lying on the road, you included, they're busted and out of the race.

Traffic. Watch for cars, and for oncoming traffic on the other side of the road. A head-on crash hurts your bike. At crossroads the traffic lights change: on red, cars stop, cross traffic drives through and people walk across. You can run a red, but watch for cars crossing. At railway crossings the barriers drop and the lights flash before a train comes through; don't race it. Trains also run on tracks beside some roads, and trams share the city streets.

Bars. RIDER stamina drops when you're hit. BIKE condition drops when you crash. Wreck the bike and the race is over.

Weather. Pick it on the road-select screen. All Seasons changes the weather at random as you ride. Any other choice lasts the whole race, and only day and night change, at random. In Thick Fog and Blizzard you can barely see; look for headlights and tail lights. In Fairbanks, Sisimiut and Longyearbyen the road is always icy.

Zombie Escape. No cars, just zombies, and the cop. Ride through them at speed. Slow down or fall near them and they'll pile on top of you, and that's the end. The cop gets eaten too if he stops or falls.

Volcano Lava Escape. A volcano is erupting and a wall of lava is rolling down the road behind you. Keep moving, because if it catches you, it's over. Steer around the lava streams across the road and the glowing pools where lava bombs land. Ride into lava and you melt, and the race is over.

You can ride off the road onto fields, sand and snow, but it's slower; watch for trees and rocks. Set your name and outfit under Rider, and pick any bike in the Garage. Choose Vintage Classic or Real-World HD graphics on the road-select screen.

On a phone the throttle is automatic. Use the buttons to steer, brake, punch and kick.
